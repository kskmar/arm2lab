01 / ABOUT THE LAB

Autonomy meets the real world.

ARM²Lab is the Autonomous Robotic Manipulation & Multi-Agent Systems Lab, co-led by Dr Maria Koskinopoulou and Dr Ignacio Carlucho. We bring together robot perception, learning, control and hardware to tackle tasks where robots must adapt to changing conditions and work safely alongside people and other robots.
