Research

Our work spans foundational methods and experiments on physical robotic systems.

Medical robotics

Robot perception and sensing for image-guided interventions and precise manipulation in healthcare.

Robotic manipulation & assembly

Planning, vision and control for handling objects and assembling delicate components.

Disassembly & sustainability

Adaptive perception and planning for taking apart complex products and recovering materials.

Multi-agent & marine robotics

Learning and coordination for autonomous systems working together in dynamic environments, including underwater missions.
